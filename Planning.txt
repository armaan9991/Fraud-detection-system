Fraud System

what problem we are solving?

detect suspicious financial transactions using ML and business rule.


Users :
Bank
admins,
automated systems

what is system doing:

accept transactions, analyze risk , flag suspicious activities , store fraud , show dashboard


Function Requirements

user can make a transaction, predict fraud probability, store transaction history, view fraud alerts, logs suspicious activities.
